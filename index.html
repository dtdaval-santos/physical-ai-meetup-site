The Format

What happens at a Physical AI Meetup

Every chapter follows the same proven format — built for real conversation, not just talks.

Networking & Pizza

Informal time for attendees, speakers and sponsors to connect before the talks start.

Community Intro & Shoutouts

Welcome remarks, sponsor and venue partner acknowledgement, and collaborator shoutouts.

Expert Tech Talks

~3 speakers per event, 20-minute talks plus Q&A, on real-world physical AI deployments.

Open Mic — Demos & Showcases

Community members demo their own projects and builds to the room.
